About AI extraction

When Ollama is running, each imported listing is processed in the background to extract extra fields and generate a short summary:

  • Pets allowed, internet included
  • Elevator, renovation status, WG-suitability
  • Key money (Ablöse), lease type
  • Year built, laundry available
  • German summary (2–3 sentences)

Extraction runs ~30–60 seconds after import as a background job. Fields already found by the parser are never overwritten.

Model recommendations for CPU-only servers:
llama3.2:1b — fast (~35s), good quality
llama3.2:3b — slower (~90s), better quality
qwen2:0.5b — very fast (~10s), lower quality
